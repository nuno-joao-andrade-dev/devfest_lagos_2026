## Post-Workshop Community Recommendations for Attendees
After completing the hands-on Genkit session at DevFest Lagos:
1. **Sunset Networking Walk:** Walk the **Passadiços da Ponta da Piedade** starting 45 minutes before sunset from *Praia do Camilo* to the *Farol da Ponta da Piedade* (1913 lighthouse).
2. **Pastry Stop:** Stop by a traditional *pastelaria* near **Praça Gil Eanes** or **Rua 25 de Abril** to try an authentic foil-wrapped **Dom Rodrigo** paired with a *bica* (espresso) or *medronho* liqueur.
